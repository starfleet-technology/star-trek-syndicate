# Star Trek: Syndicate 

> *"The line between hero and villain isn't as clear as Starfleet would have you believe. Out here, in the shadows between the stars, survival means making choices the Academy never prepared you for."*

A fan-made mod for Modiphius Star Trek Adventures RPG, exploring the dark shades of the universe — where latinum speaks louder than ideals, and the Federation's light casts long shadows.

---

## 🌌 About

Star Trek: Syndicate is a comprehensive modification for the Star Trek Adventures role-playing game, designed to immerse players in the morally complex and often perilous world of interstellar syndicates and shadowy dealings. This mod introduces new rules, character options, and narrative elements that allow players to explore the darker side of the Star Trek universe.

[:fontawesome-solid-paper-plane: Explore more about the concept](#){ .md-button }

---

## 📦 What You'll Need

Before diving into the shadows, make sure you have:

- **Star Trek Adventures** (2nd Edition recommended) — This mod builds upon the official ruleset
- **Dice** — The standard 2d20 system dice (d6s and d20s)
- **Character sheets** — Modified versions provided in this supplement
- **A crew of morally flexible players** — 3-6 recommended
- **A Game Master** — Someone willing to weave tales of intrigue and moral compromise

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

To embark on your journey into the shadows of the galaxy, start with the **Core Rules** module. This will provide you with the essential mechanics and options needed for Syndicate-style play. From there, you can explore additional modules to enrich your experience.

!!! warning "Important Notice"

    *Star Trek: Syndicate* is a *modification*, not a standalone game. 
    You'll need the official **Star Trek Adventures** rules to play.
