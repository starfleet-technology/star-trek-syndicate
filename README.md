# Star Trek: Syndicate

> *"The line between hero and villain isn't as clear as Starfleet would have you believe. Out here, in the shadows between the stars, survival means making choices the Academy never prepared you for."*

A fan-made mod for Modiphius Star Trek Adventures RPG, exploring the dark shades of the universe — where latinum speaks louder than ideals, and the Federation's light casts long shadows.

## 📦 What You'll Need

Before diving into the shadows, make sure you have:

- **Star Trek Adventures Core Rulebook** (2nd Edition recommended) — This mod builds upon the official ruleset
- **Dice** — The standard 2d20 system dice (d6s and d20s)
- **Character sheets** — Modified versions provided in this supplement
- **A crew of morally flexible players** — 3-6 recommended
- **A Game Master** — Someone willing to weave tales of intrigue and moral compromise

> ⚠️ **Important:** Star Trek Syndicate is a *modification*, not a standalone game. You'll need the base Star Trek Adventures rules to play.

---

## 🌌 About the Game

Star Trek Syndicate takes you beyond the pristine corridors of starships and into the gritty underbelly of the Star Trek universe. Play as smugglers, information brokers, reformed criminals, Section 31 operatives, or anyone else who operates in the grey areas where Federation ideals meet harsh reality.

This isn't about boldly going — it's about *carefully going*, watching your back, and deciding just how far you're willing to compromise your principles for survival, profit, or the greater good.
