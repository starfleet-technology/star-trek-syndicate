    The Dossier

    Standing order, posted above every ops terminal

    "Accurate intel wins jobs. Bad intel gets you killed."

    The Dossier is your at-table reference. Everything here is written to be consulted, not read. Find the entry, get the value, close the page, continue the session.

    All mechanical definitions, thresholds, stat blocks, tables, and canonical rule text live here. When another document references a rule, it links here. This is the authoritative source.
