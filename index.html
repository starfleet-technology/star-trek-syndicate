    Star Trek: Syndicate

    Someone, some time ago

    "The line between hero and villain isn't as clear as Starfleet would have you believe. Out here, in the shadows between the stars, survival means making choices the Academy never prepared you for."

    A fan-made mod for Modiphius Star Trek Adventures RPG, exploring the dark shades of the universe — where latinum speaks louder than ideals, and the Federation's light casts long shadows.

    About

    Star Trek: Syndicate is a comprehensive modification for the Star Trek Adventures role-playing game, designed to immerse players in the morally complex and often perilous world of interstellar syndicates and shadowy dealings. This mod introduces new rules, character options, and narrative elements that allow players to explore the darker side of the Star Trek universe.

    Read more about the concept

    What You'll Need

    Before diving into the shadows, make sure you have:

    • Star Trek Adventures

      This mod builds upon the official ruleset

    • Dice

      The standard 2d20 system dice (2 d20s)

    • Some morally flexible players

      3-6 recommended

    • A Game Master

      Someone willing to weave tales of intrigue and moral compromise

    There is a lot of terrific content for the official Star Trek Adventures RPG, so make sure to:

    Explore the Modiphius shop

    Singleplayer in the works!

    While Star Trek: Syndicate is designed for group play, we're working on some neat rules and guidance for singleplayer campaigns. Stay tuned for updates!

    Getting Started

    To embark on your journey into the shadows of the galaxy, start with the Quickstart Guide for an instant setup. This will provide you with the essential mechanics and options needed for Syndicate-style play.

    From there, you can explore the additional modules to enrich your experience.

    Important Notice

    Star Trek: Syndicate is a modification, not a standalone game. You'll need the official Star Trek Adventures rules to play.
